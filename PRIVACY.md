# Privacy Notice — Nimble Arcade

**Last updated:** 23 October 2026 · applies to version 1.2.1
**Site:** https://beradiant.github.io/nimble-arcade/
**Operator:** Aalok Kumar Pandey

## Summary

- **No account or sign-up.** Nimble Arcade never asks who you are. Any name you enter is only shown back to you.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Scores, settings, your avatar and toy settings are saved in your browser's local storage and are never sent to the operator or anyone else.
- **No tracking.** The app uses no cookies, analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, or third-party scripts, fonts or images. All graphics, fonts and sounds are built into the app.
- **Your photos stay yours.** Images you load into the Jigsaw game are processed inside your browser and never uploaded.

## Hosting by GitHub Pages

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a static hosting service provided by GitHub, Inc. When your browser requests the site, GitHub's servers receive the technical information any website receives, such as your IP address, browser user agent, the page requested and the time of the request. GitHub may record this for security and to operate the service.

The operator of Nimble Arcade has no access to these server logs and adds no tracking of its own. GitHub's practices are described in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Information stored on your device

| Category | What it contains | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | Name, theme, accent color, avatar | Browser local storage for this site |
| Records | Best scores, recent runs, play counts, favorites, day streak | Browser local storage for this site |
| Settings | Sound, vibration, controls, toy options | Browser local storage for this site |
| Upgrade backup | One copy of your previous data, made when the app updates its storage format | Browser local storage for this site |

The operator cannot see this data. It does not sync between browsers or devices.

**To delete it,** use **Settings → Reset account** in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

## Device features

The following are used only on your device and only while you use the app:
- vibration and haptics
- sound playback
- game controllers
- the browser's own install-to-home-screen feature

Nimble Arcade does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files (except a photo you choose for Jigsaw) or motion sensors.

## Children

Nimble Arcade collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
