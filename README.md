# Nimble Arcade

**40 small games. 11 fidget toys. One playful little universe.**

### [Play Nimble Arcade](https://beradiant.github.io/nimble-arcade/)

Created by **Aalok Kumar Pandey**.

Nimble is a collection of quick games and tactile toys, with a shared visual language, original artwork, sound and the Nimble Dots typeface.

- Reflex, memory, focus, puzzle and arcade games
- Eleven fidget toys
- Touch, mouse and keyboard controls, plus Pocket mode
- Themes, accents and a personal avatar
- Practice, tutorials, personal bests and local records
- No accounts, advertising or analytics

Open the site on your phone, tablet or computer. You can also add it to your home screen.

## About this repository

This repository distributes the production website. The original source, development tools and development history are maintained privately. Production assets are optimized and obfuscated; source maps and editable source bundles are not published.

## Privacy

Your scores, settings and uploaded puzzle photos stay on your device. See [the privacy notice](PRIVACY.md) for hosting details.
